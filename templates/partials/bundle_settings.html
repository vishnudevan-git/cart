These rules decide how {{ buyer_label }} may order from you. A bundle is only a count: a bundle of 10 is 10 units of your stock. The most specific rule wins: product, then supplier, then global. Customers always buy singles.
  • Singles allowed: buyers order any count. You break bundles.
  • Bundle only: buyers order whole bundles only.
  • Follow upstream: use {{ forward_label }}'s setting, and keep no loose stock of your own.
